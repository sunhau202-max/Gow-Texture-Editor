========================================================================
 INFORMATION  -  GOW TEXTURE EDITOR  v1.0
========================================================================

WHAT IT CAN DO
  - Replace textures inside God of War 1 and God of War 2 (PS2) WADs: export PNG,
    replace from PNG, preview.
  - Open GoW 1 and GoW 2 ISOs (PART1.PAK / PART2.PAK -> WAD list through GODOFWAR.TOC), a
    single WAD, or a folder of WADs; save straight into the ISO with a backup and Restore ISO.
  - Two texture kinds: 8-bit (256 colors) and 4-bit (16 colors).
  - Three languages: Indonesia, English, Português (Brasil).

NUMBERS (scan results used to build this tool)
  - GoW 1: 216 WADs; 6,623 linked textures; 6,591 editable.
  - GoW 2: 292 WADs; 8,427 linked textures; 8,327 editable.
  Coverage is limited to the WADs the author scanned. Other WADs cannot be guaranteed.

WHAT IT CANNOT DO / OUT OF SCOPE
  - Changing anything other than textures inside a WAD (3D models, audio, text, etc.).
  - Rebuilding ISOs, changing a WAD's size, or opening loose PART*.PAK files outside an ISO.
  - VPK, VAG, PSS/PSW and other non-WAD files (hidden).
  - A small share of textures: other color formats (16-bit or several images in one
    texture) and non-standard sizes. They are marked "unsupported".

TECHNICAL NOTES
  - The WAD size never changes; only the texture data is overwritten.
  - WAD location in the ISO: GoW 1 uses a PART number + sector; GoW 2 uses a location table (it
    may hold more than one copy). Every copy is written; the backup is NAME.iso.texbak.
  - 8-bit textures use the PS2 pixel layout (swizzle) and a 256-color palette in GS order;
    4-bit textures are linear. The tool handles this automatically.
  - Shared / dual palettes: the original palette is kept (image colors are mapped to it).
  - The language choice is stored in texture_tools_settings.json; errors are logged to
    texture_tool_error.txt (both next to the exe).

TEST STATUS
  Tested automatically: 14,918 textures were read and written back with byte-for-byte
  identical results; PNGs are read and written back without differences; replacing with the
  same image gives the same texture; only the selected texture's data changes. The ISO/TOC reader was tested with simulated ISOs built from the real GoW 1 and GoW 2 GODOFWAR.TOC files and real WADs (WAD lists, reading, writing every copy, backup and restore all matched). The author has NOT yet tested this version on a real game ISO or in the game. Use with care and always on a copy.
